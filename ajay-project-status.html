How this is calculated
  • Blue, In progress: Design In Progress, Design, Dev In Progress, In Progress.
  • Green, Completed: Completed, Deployed.
  • Yellow, Needs discussion / on hold: Discussion Required or Awaiting Action.
  • Grey, Not started: Not Started. Tasks with an empty Status cell are shown as “No status”.
  • Blue also covers Design and R&D.
  • Due this week: tasks that are not completed and not in a discussion / awaiting status, with a Follow Up Date from today (Sep 29) through Sun, Oct 4 (past-due dates are included and flagged).
  • Needs discussion: every task whose Status is Discussion Required, Awaiting Action or On Hold, whatever its date. A task never appears in both boxes.
  • Priority: shown as a tag next to the task when the Priority column is filled; the table can sort by it.
  • Table view: sort by any column. Tasks with no due date stay at the bottom when sorting by date.
  • Comments are shown as written in the Comments column (dated 09/29 unless noted).
